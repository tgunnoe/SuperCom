# Simulation specification

This file specifies the v0 simulation rules of SuperCom: tick model, economy, build mechanics, categories and tech tiers, orders, weapons and projectiles, damage and armour and shields, health and veterancy and death and wreckage, movement layers, intel, victory, and initial state. Each clause names its evaluator. A clause whose evaluator is a script is machine-verified: the script reads only `SUPERCOM_FA_DIR`, reads archive members in memory, prints counts, constants, and identifiers, prints `SKIP:` and succeeds when the install is absent, fails under `SUPERCOM_REQUIRE_GAME=1`, and exits non-zero when its clause is false. A clause whose evaluator is a pinned source is cited, not run; it is agent-checked. Counts below are those of the installed Forged Alliance data (568 unit blueprints in `units.scd`). Statements about the original engine rest on a pinned citation or a script-verified data fact; a choice of this project is a design clause and says so. The engine-API inventory at the end classifies every engine-exposed function and every Lua-side behaviour group.

## Tick model

**SIM-001** `reference` `source:https://raw.githubusercontent.com/FAForever/fa/041f661457b0/engine/User.lua#L572`
The nominal simulation rate of the original engine MUST be taken as 10 simulation ticks per second: FAF's engine stub documents GetSimTicksPerSecond as returning a hardcoded constant of how many sim ticks are supposed to occur per second and annotates its return as 10. The stub states no value for SecondsPerTick, GetGameTimeSeconds, or GetGameTick, so this clause is the only source of the rate.

**SIM-002** `design` `source:docs/adr/0003-render-and-sim-stack.md`
The sim's tick length constant MUST be 0.1 seconds of simulated time per tick, derived from SIM-001 as the reciprocal of 10 ticks per second, and simulated time MUST be held as an integer tick count, never as a wall-clock reading. This confirms the 10 Hz target that the stack decision left to the specification.

**SIM-003** `game-data` `script:tools/spec_eval/sim/tick.py SIM-003`
The installed lua.scd MUST reference the tick accessors in exactly this pattern: GetSimTicksPerSecond in 1 member (lua/ui/game/economy.lua), SecondsPerTick in 1 member (lua/sim/tasks/EnhanceTask.lua), GetGameTimeSeconds in 10 members, and GetGameTick in none; 12 members reference at least one of the first three.

**SIM-004** `engine-stub` `source:https://raw.githubusercontent.com/FAForever/fa/041f661457b0/engine/Sim.lua#L643`
The sim MUST expose the current tick as an integer count, as the stub declares GetGameTick at line 643 of engine/Sim.lua with an integer return, and MUST expose elapsed simulated time in seconds and the seconds-per-tick constant as numbers, as the stub declares GetGameTimeSeconds (engine/Core.lua line 237) and SecondsPerTick (engine/Core.lua line 418). The stubs fix shape only.

**SIM-005** `design` `source:docs/adr/0003-render-and-sim-stack.md`
The realised wall-clock duration of a tick at each game speed is unmeasured and no source states it, so the sim MUST NOT depend on it and the application layer MAY pace ticks by any policy; this is a low-priority clause whose signpost is a comparison of replay tick counts against wall-clock duration at each game speed. It never qualifies the nominal rate of SIM-001.

## Economy and build rules

**SIM-040** `design` `test:sim_stall_grants_fraction_and_clamps_storage`
In the economy phase each army's requests MUST be settled against what is available, stored plus income: the fraction of a resource granted is 1 when nothing of it is requested and otherwise the lesser of 1 and the available amount over the requested amount; an entity that requests several resources MUST be granted the lowest of its resources' fractions of each request, so a unit short of one resource consumes the other at the same fraction; the stored amount after the tick is the available amount less the amount granted, never above the army's storage and never below 0; and the stall counter MUST increase by one in every tick in which any army's fraction of any resource is below 1. The original's arithmetic is unreadable and its behaviour under a shortage is unobserved, so the rule is this project's choice, recorded in `docs/adr/0011-economy-and-build.md`. The test stalls two builders on 10 stored mass against 16 requested and an energy-only consumer beside them, and compares the fractions, the grants, the stores, the counter, and the next tick.

**SIM-041** `derived` `test:sim_extractor_requires_a_mass_marker`
Following from SIM-054 and SIM-049, a unit kind whose build restriction is OnMassDeposit MUST be placed only within 1 world unit, exclusive, on each of x, y, and z of a Mass marker that no other entity of such a kind holds, as the installed extractor code compares its position with a marker's, and the kind's mass production MUST be its blueprint value, the installed factor of marker amount over 100 being 1 on every installed marker (SIM-054), so the sim's marker carries no amount. The test refuses a site off every marker, on a Hydrocarbon marker, and exactly 1 unit from a Mass marker, accepts a site half a unit away, refuses a second extractor on a held marker, and compares the completed extractor's income and maintenance request in the ledger.

**SIM-042** `design` `test:sim_hydrocarbon_plant_on_marker`
A unit kind whose build restriction is OnHydrocarbonDeposit MUST be placed by the rule of SIM-041 against Hydrocarbon markers, and MUST produce its blueprint energy; no installed Lua states a placement or a scaling rule for a hydrocarbon plant (SIM-054), so mirroring the extractor rule is this project's choice, recorded in `docs/adr/0011-economy-and-build.md`. The test repeats SIM-041's cases with Hydrocarbon markers.

**SIM-043** `derived` `test:sim_mass_fabricator_scales_with_energy_fraction`
Following from SIM-053, a complete unit's production MUST be multiplied by the fraction of its request granted in the last economy phase, because the installed code that divides the mass production by the consumed fraction exists to cancel the engine's own scaling, and a mass fabricator MUST keep requesting its maintenance energy under a shortage, never pausing itself, because the auto-pause identifiers occur in no installed member (SIM-053). The test starves a fabricator of energy and compares its income, its fraction, and its request over three ticks.

**SIM-056** `derived` `test:sim_build_consumption_rates_from_costs`
Following from SIM-052, SIM-055, and SIM-006, with every modifier 0 and every adjustment factor 1, the time to build a kind MUST be the larger of its BuildTime and 0.1 divided by the builder's rate, and its mass and energy rates per second MUST be the larger of the cost and 1 divided by that time; a kind without a BuildTime cannot be built; maintenance adds MaintenanceConsumptionPerSecondEnergy; and the MinConsumption floor is moot, no blueprint carrying either key (SIM-055). The test builds kinds of cost 36 and 360 at rate 10, a build time of 0.05, a cost of 0, no build time, and rates of 0 and below, and compares each result.

**SIM-057** `derived` `test:sim_economy_rates_apply_per_tick_as_a_tenth`
Following from SIM-001, SIM-002, and SIM-052, every per-second economic rate MUST apply per tick as the value divided by 10 in fixed point, rounded as the type's division rounds, because ten ticks make a second; the 0.1 floor of SIM-052 is the separate constant of raw bits 429496730 and differs from one tenth of 1 by a raw unit. The reading of the original's per-tick scaling is an inference the premises support, not an observation. The test compares the raw bits of 6, 1, and 0.3 divided by 10 with the integer quotients.

**SIM-058** `design` `test:sim_income_and_storage_settle_each_tick`
An army's storage MUST be the sum of its complete units' kind storage, its income the sum over its complete units of the kind's production per tick times the unit's last granted fraction, and both MUST be counted from completion only; the economy phase MUST record the income, the requested, the granted, and the two fractions of its last settlement in the army's ledger, and clamp the stored amounts to storage. The installed Lua activates production at a unit's creation (SIM-053), and what the engine credits for an unfinished unit is unobserved, so crediting complete units only is this project's choice, recorded in `docs/adr/0011-economy-and-build.md`. The test settles a commander and a storage unit complete and under construction and compares the stores, the clamp, and the ledger.

**SIM-059** `design` `test:sim_build_progress_and_assist_sum_rates`
An entity under construction MUST gain, in each build phase after the one that started it, the sum over its contributors, the builder and every entity guarding it or its builder, each within the build range of SIM-061, of the contributor's granted fraction times its build rate per tick, in build-rate-seconds; it MUST complete when the work reaches its build time (floored as SIM-056), its health tracking its maximum times its progress until then, and completion MUST count in builds completed, pop the builder's order, and clear the contributors' work. Only a complete entity MAY contribute work or request build resources, and an unfinished entity of a kind with a build rate MUST neither start a build nor take a Guard assignment, its commands queueing and waiting until it completes; a kind without a build time MUST NOT be spawned under construction. A command applied with the Replace mode MUST clear the work of each entity it names, as a Stop does, so the replaced target gains and costs nothing from that tick, and a work item naming an entity that is gone or complete MUST be cleared at the start of the build phase, so the builder tries its head order again. The original's sum of several builders is unreadable and its behaviour unobserved, so the rule is this project's choice, recorded in `docs/adr/0011-economy-and-build.md`. The test completes a build alone at tick 60, with a rate-5 assister at tick 40, and with an assister out of range at tick 60, and compares the work and the health halfway.

**SIM-060** `design` `test:sim_factory_queue_builds_in_order_at_its_position`
A factory MUST work its BuildFactory orders one at a time in queue order, spawning a mobile unit under construction at the factory's position in the build phase that starts it and starting the next in the build phase that completes the last; an order naming a kind outside the factory's buildable set, a kind that is not mobile, or a kind without a build time MUST be dropped and counted as ignored. An unfinished factory MUST work none of its orders until it is complete. A Replace to a working factory MUST end the work on the unit in progress, which stays unfinished in place, and the new head MUST start in that tick's build phase. Where a unit appears and how a queue advances are unobserved, so the rule is this project's choice, recorded in `docs/adr/0011-economy-and-build.md`. The test queues two units on a factory of rate 20 and compares the spawn position, the completion ticks 50 and 100, and the dropped order.

**SIM-061** `design` `test:sim_build_site_rules`
A BuildMobile order MUST start only when the kind is a structure in the builder's buildable set with a build time and land in its build layers, every cell of its footprint, the kind's footprint defaulting to one cell when the block or a dimension is missing or zero, centred on the site, lies inside the map, is land-passable (SIM-048), and is covered by no other entity's footprint, a mobile unit covering the cell of its position, and the builder is within 5 world units of the footprint's nearest edge; an order failing a kind or site check MUST be dropped when it reaches the head of the queue and counted as ignored, an order out of range MUST wait at the head, only a complete builder MUST start a build, and a Stop reaching the head, like a command in the Replace mode, MUST clear the builder's work and leave the unfinished entity in place. The ten stated MaxBuildDistance values (SIM-055) and MaxGroundVariation are deviated from, not read, there is no placement on water in this version, and the original's rules are unobserved, so these are this project's choices, recorded in `docs/adr/0011-economy-and-build.md`. The test covers each case with one assertion.

**SIM-048** `design` `test:sim_land_passability_from_heightmap`

**SIM-049** `design` `test:sim_layer_set_and_caps_parse`

**SIM-050** `design` `test:sim_ingest_rounds_to_nearest_q_and_refuses_overflow`
Every blueprint scalar the sim holds as a fixed-point number MUST be converted once at ingest to the nearest representable value, ties to even, and a value of magnitude 2^31 or more, or not finite, MUST be an ingest error naming the field's path, never a wrap or a saturation; heightmap samples MUST convert exactly, which requires a scale that is a power of two no smaller than 2^-16 (every installed map stores 2^-7); geometry stays binary64 (ADR-0006); the rule is recorded in `docs/adr/0010-ingest-and-initial-state.md`. The test converts 0.1 and compares the raw bits with the nearest value, and refuses 2^31, a negative magnitude over it, and NaN. A second test, `sim_ingest_rounds_ties_to_even_and_reads_rulebr_none`, converts 2^-33 and 3 times 2^-33 and compares the raw bits 0 and 2, and checks that an explicit RULEUBR_None build restriction reads as none.

**SIM-051** `design` `test:sim_skirmish_places_commanders_and_starts_full`
