# Simulation specification

This file specifies the v0 simulation rules of SuperCom: tick model, economy, build mechanics, categories and tech tiers, orders, weapons and projectiles, damage and armour and shields, health and veterancy and death and wreckage, movement layers, intel, victory, and initial state. Each clause names its evaluator. A clause whose evaluator is a script is machine-verified: the script reads only `SUPERCOM_FA_DIR`, reads archive members in memory, prints counts, constants, and identifiers, prints `SKIP:` and succeeds when the install is absent, fails under `SUPERCOM_REQUIRE_GAME=1`, and exits non-zero when its clause is false. A clause whose evaluator is a pinned source is cited, not run; it is agent-checked. Counts below are those of the installed Forged Alliance data (568 unit blueprints in `units.scd`). Statements about the original engine rest on a pinned citation or a script-verified data fact; a choice of this project is a design clause and says so. The engine-API inventory at the end classifies every engine-exposed function and every Lua-side behaviour group.

## Tick model

**SIM-001** `reference` `source:https://raw.githubusercontent.com/FAForever/fa/041f661457b0/engine/User.lua#L572`
The nominal simulation rate of the original engine MUST be taken as 10 simulation ticks per second: FAF's engine stub documents GetSimTicksPerSecond as returning a hardcoded constant of how many sim ticks are supposed to occur per second and annotates its return as 10. The stub states no value for SecondsPerTick, GetGameTimeSeconds, or GetGameTick, so this clause is the only source of the rate.

**SIM-002** `design` `source:docs/adr/0003-render-and-sim-stack.md`
The sim's tick length constant MUST be 0.1 seconds of simulated time per tick, derived from SIM-001 as the reciprocal of 10 ticks per second, and simulated time MUST be held as an integer tick count, never as a wall-clock reading. This confirms the 10 Hz target that the stack decision left to the specification.

**SIM-003** `game-data` `script:tools/spec_eval/sim/tick.py SIM-003`
The installed lua.scd MUST reference the tick accessors in exactly this pattern: GetSimTicksPerSecond in 1 member (lua/ui/game/economy.lua), SecondsPerTick in 1 member (lua/sim/tasks/EnhanceTask.lua), GetGameTimeSeconds in 10 members, and GetGameTick in none; 12 members reference at least one of the first three.

**SIM-004** `engine-stub` `source:https://raw.githubusercontent.com/FAForever/fa/041f661457b0/engine/Sim.lua#L643`
The sim MUST expose the current tick as an integer count, as the stub declares GetGameTick at line 643 of engine/Sim.lua with an integer return, and MUST expose elapsed simulated time in seconds and the seconds-per-tick constant as numbers, as the stub declares GetGameTimeSeconds (engine/Core.lua line 237) and SecondsPerTick (engine/Core.lua line 418). The stubs fix shape only.

**SIM-005** `design` `source:docs/adr/0003-render-and-sim-stack.md`
The realised wall-clock duration of a tick at each game speed is unmeasured and no source states it, so the sim MUST NOT depend on it and the application layer MAY pace ticks by any policy; this is a low-priority clause whose signpost is a comparison of replay tick counts against wall-clock duration at each game speed. It never qualifies the nominal rate of SIM-001.

## Economy

## Build mechanics

**SIM-010** `game-data` `script:tools/spec_eval/sim/build.py SIM-010`
The set of buildable units MUST be driven by data: 105 blueprints define a non-empty Economy.BuildableCategory, holding 215 expressions with 68 distinct identifiers and no operator character, so each expression is a whitespace-separated list of identifiers; 36 identifiers are blueprint IDs in lower case rather than category strings, and 2 (CYBRANMOBILEMISSILE, URL0001UPGRADE) are neither.

**SIM-011** `derived` `script:tools/spec_eval/sim/build.py SIM-011`
Following from SIM-010 and the category vocabulary of SIM-013, the sim MUST decide what a builder can build by reading each BuildableCategory expression as the intersection of its identifiers, an identifier matching a blueprint when it is one of that blueprint's category strings or its blueprint ID compared in lower case. Under this reading 370 of 568 blueprints are selected by at least one builder and 198 by none, every blueprint that carries a BUILTBY category is selected, none of the 4 COMMAND blueprints is, and 14 expressions select no blueprint.

**SIM-012** `engine-stub` `source:https://raw.githubusercontent.com/FAForever/fa/041f661457b0/engine/Sim/Unit.lua#L156`
The sim's unit interface MUST provide the build accessors that the Unit stub declares: Unit:CanBuild (line 104), Unit:GetBuildRate (156), Unit:SetBuildRate (524), Unit:SetConsumptionActive (537), and Unit:SetProductionActive (619).

**SIM-017** `engine-stub` `source:https://raw.githubusercontent.com/FAForever/fa/041f661457b0/engine/Sim.lua#L760`

**SIM-018** `design` `source:AGENTS.md`
A simulation command MUST name the issuing army, the unit entity IDs it addresses, one order of the SIM-016 vocabulary, and whether it replaces or appends to the unit's queue; the sim MUST ignore a command that addresses a unit the issuing army does not own, and MUST apply the commands of one tick in the total order the command stream fixes. This is the project's command-stream choice (R2).

## Damage, armour, and shields

**SIM-022** `game-data` `script:tools/spec_eval/sim/damage.py SIM-022`
Armour MUST be a table of damage-type multipliers: lua/armordefinition.lua of lua.scd defines 6 armour entries holding 10 multiplier strings, namely Default, Normal, and Light with Normal 1.0 only, Commander with Normal 1.0, Overcharge 0.033333, and Deathnuke 0.05, Structure with Normal 1.0, Overcharge 0.066666, and Deathnuke 0.01, and Experimental with ExperimentalFootfall 0.0 only. **SIM-023** `game-data` `script:tools/spec_eval/sim/damage.py SIM-023`

**SIM-024** `game-data` `script:tools/spec_eval/sim/damage.py SIM-024`

## Movement and pathfinding layers

**SIM-029** `game-data` `script:tools/spec_eval/sim/movement.py SIM-029`
Movement MUST be selected by Physics.MotionType, which every blueprint defines with 8 distinct values: RULEUMT_None 374, RULEUMT_Air 60, RULEUMT_Land 50, RULEUMT_Water 27, RULEUMT_Hover 19, RULEUMT_Amphibious 17, RULEUMT_SurfacingSub 13, and RULEUMT_AmphibiousFloating 8.

**SIM-030** `game-data` `script:tools/spec_eval/sim/movement.py SIM-030`
Placement layers MUST be the six keys of Physics.BuildOnLayerCaps, LAYER_Air, LAYER_Land, LAYER_Orbit, LAYER_Seabed, LAYER_Sub, and LAYER_Water, present in all 568 blueprints; 402 blueprints define a top-level Footprint, in 24 distinct SizeX and SizeZ pairs; lua/sim/Unit.lua of lua.scd defines OnLayerChange, OnMotionHorzEventChange, OnMotionVertEventChange, OnMotionTurnEventChange, and OnTerrainTypeChange.

**SIM-033** `design` `source:AGENTS.md`
Path planning MUST be done per MotionType of SIM-029 over a passability grid per movement layer, derived from the map's terrain and water heights, and MUST be deterministic, breaking ties by cell index; the planning algorithm is this project's choice (R2).

## Victory and initial state

**SIM-034** `game-data` `script:tools/spec_eval/sim/victory.py SIM-034`
The match result MUST be decided by a victory option: lua/victory.lua of lua.scd names the options demoralization, domination, and eradication (and no sandbox option), reads ScenarioInfo.Options.Victory in 3 places, and defines CheckVictory and CallEndGame.

**SIM-035** `game-data` `script:tools/spec_eval/sim/victory.py SIM-035`
A skirmish MUST start with one commander per army, drawn from exactly the 4 COMMAND blueprints UEL0001 (UEF), UAL0001 (Aeon), URL0001 (Cybran), and XSL0001 (Seraphim); the Lua side starts armies through InitializeArmies, CreateInitialArmyGroup, CreateResources, and CommanderWarpDelay in lua/sim/ScenarioUtilities.lua of mohodata.scd and through OnCreateHuman, OnCreateAI, OnSpawnPreBuiltUnits, InitializeEconomyState, OnDefeat, OnVictory, and OnDraw in lua/aibrain.lua of lua.scd.

**SIM-036** `design` `source:AGENTS.md`
One tick MUST be a pure function of the sim state and that tick's command batch: the sim visits entities in ascending entity ID, draws random numbers only from the seeded generator held in the state, and never reads the clock, the operating system's randomness, or the iteration order of an unordered container (R2, I2).
