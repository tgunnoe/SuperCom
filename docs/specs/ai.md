# Skirmish AI specification

This file specifies what the simulation exposes to an AI army, the minimum behaviour of the v0 built-in AI, and the facts about the installed game's AI data that bear on both. It follows the clause grammar of docs/specs/README.md. A clause of class `design` is a decision of this project and states nothing about how the original engine or its AI behaves; a clause of class `game-data` is a fact about the installed Forged Alliance data and is machine-verified by a script that reads only `SUPERCOM_FA_DIR`, reads archive members in memory, prints counts, constants, and identifiers, prints `SKIP:` and succeeds when the install is absent, fails under `SUPERCOM_REQUIRE_GAME=1`, and exits non-zero when its clause is false. A clause whose evaluator is a pinned source is cited, not run; it is agent-checked. Clauses cite the simulation specification (docs/specs/sim.md) by clause ID, and the HUD specification (docs/specs/ui.md) where the two share a view of the sim.

Whether the v0 AI ships as built-in Rust or as a first-party Wasm plugin is the unknown U8 of AGENTS.md. This file does not decide it (SKAI-015). The exact set of percepts the sim publishes, the AI's per-faction and per-map behaviour, difficulty, and the numeric values of the budget in SKAI-014 are frontiers: they are named where they arise, and nothing here invents them.

## The AI's interface to the sim

**SKAI-001** `design` `source:docs/specs/sim.md#SIM-018`
The only effector of an AI army MUST be the command stream of SIM-018: an AI issues the same commands a human player issues, each naming the issuing army, the unit entity IDs it addresses, one order, and whether it replaces or appends to the unit's queue, and the sim MUST validate and apply an AI army's commands by the same rules as a human army's (SIM-018 ignores a command that addresses a unit the issuing army does not own). The sim MUST NOT give an AI any other way to change the state, and in particular MUST NOT expose the in-process build and platoon calls listed in SKAI-004. The order of a command is one of the SIM-016 vocabulary or, for construction, one of the build orders of SIM-017; widening SIM-018's order field to the build orders is a frontier of the sim specification. This is the project's choice (R2).

**SKAI-002** `design` `source:docs/specs/sim.md#SIM-036`
What an AI observes MUST be the sim's published snapshot, the same read-only view the HUD reads (HUD-002 in docs/specs/ui.md): for every entity of its own army and every other entity that the intel of SIM-030 and SIM-032 reveals to that army, the blueprint ID, army, position, health, and order queue; and for its own army the economy totals, meaning the quantities SIM-009 names together with the storage of SIM-007 and SIM-008. The snapshot MUST be a function of the sim state alone (SIM-036, R2), and an AI MUST NOT read sim memory outside it. The exact percept set is a frontier of the sim implementation: this clause names the minimum fields and invents no further percept.

**SKAI-003** `engine-stub` `source:https://raw.githubusercontent.com/FAForever/fa/041f661457b0/engine/Sim/CAiBrain.lua#L363`
The shape of an AI's observation queries is fixed by the CAiBrain stub, which declares CAiBrain:GetListOfUnits (line 363), CAiBrain:GetUnitsAroundPoint (470), CAiBrain:GetNumUnitsAroundPoint (399), CAiBrain:GetCurrentUnits (290), CAiBrain:GetCurrentEnemy (284), CAiBrain:GetArmyStartPos (193), CAiBrain:GetFactionIndex (335), CAiBrain:GetThreatAtPosition (433), and CAiBrain:GetHighestThreatPosition (353), alongside the economy accessors of SIM-009. The snapshot of SKAI-002 SHOULD let an AI answer queries of those shapes (units of a category, units around a point, the current enemy, the start position, the faction, and threat at a position). The stub fixes names and arguments, not behaviour or return values.

**SKAI-004** `engine-stub` `source:https://raw.githubusercontent.com/FAForever/fa/041f661457b0/engine/Sim/CAiBrain.lua#L42`
The CAiBrain stub declares calls that build and group units in-process: CAiBrain:AssignUnitsToPlatoon (line 35; platoon, units, squad, formation), CAiBrain:BuildPlatoon (42; template, factories, count), CAiBrain:BuildStructure (50; builder, blueprintID, buildLocation, buildRelative), CAiBrain:BuildUnit (57; builder, unitToBuild, count), CAiBrain:DisbandPlatoon (109; platoon), and CAiBrain:MakePlatoon (506; name, aiPlan). The sim MUST NOT offer these as an AI's effectors, because SKAI-001 admits commands only; the stub fixes their names and arguments, not their behaviour.

## The installed game's AI data

**SKAI-005** `game-data` `script:tools/spec_eval/ai/data.py SKAI-005`
The installed game's AI data MUST be taken as the 94 members under lua/AI/ of lua.scd, which no other archive holds: 5 directory entries and 89 files, of which 9 sit directly in lua/AI/ (AIAddBuilderTable, AIBehaviors, SkirmishStrategyList, Strategy-Test, aiarchetype-managerloader, aiattackutilities, aibuildstructures, aibuildunits, aiutilities) and 80 sit in four groups. The groups hold OpAI 41 members (40 files), AIBaseTemplates 24 (23 files), AIBuilders 13 (12 files), and PlatoonTemplates 6 (5 files).

**SKAI-006** `game-data` `script:tools/spec_eval/ai/data.py SKAI-006`
The builder data MUST be taken as the 12 files of lua/AI/AIBuilders in lua.scd (AIAirAttackBuilders, AIArtilleryBuilders, AIDefenseBuilders, AIEconomicBuilders, AIEconomyUpgradeBuilders, AIExpansionBuilders, AIExperimentalBuilders, AIFactoryConstructionBuilders, AIIntelBuilders, AILandAttackBuilders, AINavalBuilders, and AISeaAttackBuilders), which together declare 141 BuilderGroup tables and 410 Builder tables, each Builder carrying a BuilderName (410 names); the counts are of the code left after comments are removed.

**SKAI-007** `game-data` `script:tools/spec_eval/ai/data.py SKAI-007`
The platoon data MUST be taken as the 5 files of lua/AI/PlatoonTemplates in lua.scd (AirPlatoonTemplates, EngineerPlatoonTemplates, LandPlatoonTemplates, SeaPlatoonTemplates, and StructurePlatoonTemplates), which together declare 127 PlatoonTemplate tables: 28 air, 21 engineer, 38 land, 15 sea, and 25 structure; the counts are of the code left after comments are removed.

**SKAI-008** `game-data` `script:tools/spec_eval/ai/data.py SKAI-008`
The builder files of SKAI-006 MUST be read as naming unit kinds through the category vocabulary of the blueprints: they refer to categories 440 times in the form categories.NAME, with 37 distinct names, and every one of the 37 is among the 152 category strings of SIM-013.

**SKAI-009** `game-data` `script:tools/spec_eval/ai/brain.py SKAI-009`
The Lua-side AI classes MUST be located in nine members of lua.scd, each the winner of its mount order: AIBrain in lua/aibrain.lua (the sim specification's inventory lists this member in its row named Brain lifecycle and results, owner lua), Platoon in lua/platoon.lua, Builder, PlatoonBuilder, FactoryBuilder, and EngineerBuilder in lua/sim/Builder.lua, and BuilderManager, EngineerManager, FactoryBuilderManager, PlatoonFormManager, StrategyManager, and BrainConditionsMonitor, each in the member of that name under lua/sim/. Together they define 12 classes, which the script checks by name.

## The v0 built-in AI

**SKAI-010** `design` `source:docs/specs/sim.md#SIM-036`
The built-in AI MUST be deterministic: its commands for a tick MUST be a function of the snapshot of SKAI-002, of its own state carried from earlier ticks (itself a function of earlier snapshots), and of draws from the seeded generator of SIM-036 when it needs a random choice, and of nothing else. It MUST NOT read the wall clock, the operating system's randomness, the filesystem, or the iteration order of an unordered container (I2), so two runs of one initial state and one command log yield identical AI commands (R2).

**SKAI-011** `design` `source:AGENTS.md`
The v0 built-in AI MUST be one AI for every faction. For each army it controls it MUST, as a minimum and by commands alone (SKAI-001), build mass extractors and energy producers, build land factories and engineers, train land combat units in its factories, and send its land combat units to attack the nearest enemy army, nearest meaning the least distance from the AI army's start position to the enemy army's start position with ties broken by ascending army index. Each build choice MUST be a role query over the builder's buildable set as SKAI-013 states. Air, naval, and experimental play, defence, intel, expansion bases, reclaim, transports, per-faction and per-map behaviour, and difficulty are frontiers: the v0 AI MAY include them and this specification does not require them.

**SKAI-012** `game-data` `script:tools/spec_eval/ai/bootstrap.py SKAI-012`
Under the reading of SIM-011 the minimum behaviour of SKAI-011 MUST be satisfiable from the commander alone in every faction. The four commanders UEL0001, UAL0001, URL0001, and XSL0001 can build 45, 45, 45, and 41 blueprints, 14 of them TECH1 in each. Among those, the TECH1 mass extractor is UEB1103, UAB1103, URB1103, and XSB1103; the TECH1 energy producers are the two blueprints numbered 1101 and 1102 of the faction's structure prefix (UEB, UAB, URB, XSB); and the TECH1 land factory is UEB0101, UAB0101, URB0101, and XSB0101. That factory can build one TECH1 engineer, UEL0105, UAL0105, URL0105, and XSL0105, and TECH1 land direct-fire mobile units numbering 3, 3, 2, and 2. The script also reproduces the totals SIM-010 and SIM-011 pin, so it reads blueprints as the sim specification does.

**SKAI-013** `derived` `script:tools/spec_eval/ai/bootstrap.py SKAI-013`
Following from SKAI-011 and SKAI-012, the v0 AI MUST choose what to build by five role queries, each a set of category strings applied to the buildable set of the builder at hand under SIM-011's reading: mass extractor (MASSEXTRACTION, TECH1), energy producer (ENERGYPRODUCTION, TECH1), land factory (FACTORY, LAND, TECH1), engineer (ENGINEER, TECH1), and land combat unit (LAND, MOBILE, TECH1, DIRECTFIRE); and it MUST NOT carry a table of blueprint IDs per faction. The five queries name no blueprint ID and no faction, yet each selects at least one blueprint in each of the four factions, and every blueprint selected carries the category of the builder's own faction (UEF, AEON, CYBRAN, SERAPHIM).

**SKAI-014** `design` `source:docs/specs/sim.md#SIM-036`
The built-in AI's decision work per tick MUST be bounded by a budget counted in deterministic units and never in time: at most 16 commands emitted and at most 1024 snapshot entity rows examined per tick. These initial values are set by this specification without measurement and are revisited by an ADR once a sim exists. When the examination budget is spent the AI MUST stop deciding for that tick and MUST resume on the next tick at the entity after the last one examined, in ascending entity ID order (SIM-036) and wrapping around, so no entity is starved.

**SKAI-015** `design` `source:AGENTS.md`
Whether the v0 built-in AI ships as built-in Rust or as a first-party Wasm plugin is the unknown U8 and MUST remain undecided by this file, which assumes neither: whichever form is chosen MUST satisfy SKAI-001, SKAI-002, and SKAI-010, and a plugin form MUST observe the snapshot and emit commands across the WIT boundary of docs/adr/0001-plugin-boundary-wasm-components.md with NaN canonicalization and no clock, random, or filesystem imports (I3). The ADR that resolves U8 follows the spec campaign.
